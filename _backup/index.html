ANOMALOUS COOLING

La Niña

The ocean exhales. Deep, frigid waters well up, bringing temporary equilibrium, yet triggering chaotic Atlantic hurricanes and relentless rainfall.

ANOMALOUS WARMING

El Niño

The ocean inhales. Trade winds falter. A massive pool of superheated water spreads eastward, igniting the atmosphere into devastating extremes.

01

The Winds Falter

For years, the reliable trade winds push warm surface water toward Indonesia. But inevitably, they weaken. The atmospheric engine stalls.

02

The Thermocline Collapses

Without the winds to hold it back, the barrier between warm surface water and the freezing abyss flattens. The ocean layers mix, raising the global baseline.

03

The Thermal Pulse

A colossal pool of heat surges eastward across the Pacific. Trillions of tons of warmed water evaporate, loading the atmosphere with terrifying potential energy.
